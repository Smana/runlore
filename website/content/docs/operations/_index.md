Running RunLore once it is installed: knowing what it is doing, working out why it did not do
something, and moving between versions safely.

- **[Observability]({{< relref "observability.md" >}})** — the structured logs and
  Prometheus-compatible metrics RunLore emits about itself: what each investigation cost, what
  recall matched, and what the curator wrote.
- **[Troubleshooting]({{< relref "troubleshooting.md" >}})** — the *"why didn't RunLore do X?"*
  page. Alerts that never became investigations, investigations that stopped early, knowledge that
  never reached the catalog, and the log line that tells you which case you are in.
- **[Upgrade & Uninstall]({{< relref "upgrade-uninstall.md" >}})** — upgrading in place, what
  survives a restart, and removing it cleanly.

Installing for the first time is covered in
[Getting Started]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started.md" >}}).
